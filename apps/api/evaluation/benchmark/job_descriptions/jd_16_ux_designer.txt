UX Designer

Company: DesignStudio Co.
Location: Portland, OR (Remote)
Type: Full-time

About the Role:
Create delightful user experiences for our products. You'll conduct user research, design interfaces, and collaborate with product and engineering teams.

Required Skills:
- 3+ years of UX/UI design experience
- Strong portfolio demonstrating user-centered design process
- Proficiency in design tools (Figma, Sketch, Adobe XD)
- Experience with user research methods (interviews, usability testing, surveys)
- Knowledge of interaction design and information architecture
- Understanding of design systems and component libraries
- Ability to create wireframes, prototypes, and high-fidelity mockups
- Excellent visual design skills
- Strong communication and presentation abilities
- Collaborative mindset working with cross-functional teams

Preferred Skills:
- Experience with prototyping tools (Framer, Principle, ProtoPie)
- Knowledge of front-end development (HTML, CSS, JavaScript)
- Familiarity with accessibility standards (WCAG)
- Experience with design thinking workshops
- Understanding of analytics and A/B testing
- Motion design skills (After Effects, Lottie)
- Experience designing for mobile platforms

Responsibilities:
- Conduct user research and synthesize insights
- Create user flows, wireframes, and prototypes
- Design high-fidelity mockups and specifications
- Conduct usability testing and iterate on feedback
- Collaborate with product managers and engineers
- Maintain and evolve design system
- Present designs to stakeholders
- Stay current with design trends and best practices

Education:
Bachelor's degree in Design, HCI, or related field, or equivalent experience

Benefits:
Competitive salary, equity, health insurance, remote work, design conference budget, creative tools stipend
