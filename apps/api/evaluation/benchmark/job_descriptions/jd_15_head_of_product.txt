Head of Product

Company: ProductLeaders Inc.
Location: New York, NY (Hybrid)
Type: Full-time, Executive

About the Role:
Lead product strategy and execution as Head of Product. You'll manage product managers, define product vision, and drive go-to-market success. Reports to CEO or CPO.

Required Skills:
- 10+ years of product management experience
- 4+ years leading product teams
- Strong track record of successful product launches
- Experience managing product managers
- Deep understanding of product development lifecycle
- Excellent strategic thinking and prioritization skills
- Strong data analysis and customer insights expertise
- Experience with B2B or B2C SaaS products
- Proven ability to influence without authority
- Outstanding communication and presentation skills

Preferred Skills:
- Technical background or engineering degree
- Experience at product-led growth companies
- Knowledge of product analytics platforms
- Familiarity with design thinking and UX research
- Experience with pricing and packaging strategies
- Track record of 0-to-1 product development
- MBA from top business school

Responsibilities:
- Define product vision, strategy, and roadmap
- Lead team of product managers
- Collaborate with engineering, design, and go-to-market teams
- Conduct market research and competitive analysis
- Drive product discovery and experimentation
- Establish product metrics and success criteria
- Present product strategy to board and investors
- Mentor and develop product talent
- Manage product budget and resource allocation
- Champion customer-centric culture

Education:
Bachelor's degree required; MBA or technical Master's preferred

Compensation:
$220K-$320K base salary, equity, executive benefits package
