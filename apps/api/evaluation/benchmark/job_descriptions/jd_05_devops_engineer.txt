DevOps Engineer

Company: InfraCloud Inc.
Location: Boston, MA (Hybrid)
Type: Full-time

About the Role:
We're looking for a DevOps Engineer to build and maintain our cloud infrastructure and CI/CD pipelines. You'll automate deployments, improve system reliability, and work closely with development teams.

Required Skills:
- 4+ years of DevOps or infrastructure experience
- Strong experience with AWS or GCP (EC2, S3, RDS, Lambda, etc.)
- Expert knowledge of Docker and Kubernetes
- Experience with infrastructure as code (Terraform or CloudFormation)
- Proficiency in scripting languages (Python, Bash, or Go)
- Experience building CI/CD pipelines (GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, or Jenkins)
- Knowledge of monitoring and logging (Prometheus, Grafana, ELK stack)
- Understanding of networking concepts (VPC, load balancers, DNS)
- Experience with configuration management (Ansible, Chef, or Puppet)
- Strong Linux/Unix system administration skills

Preferred Skills:
- Experience with service mesh (Istio, Linkerd)
- Knowledge of security best practices and compliance (SOC2, HIPAA)
- Familiarity with GitOps workflows (ArgoCD, Flux)
- Experience with database administration and backups
- Understanding of cost optimization strategies
- Knowledge of serverless architectures
- Experience with incident response and on-call duties

Responsibilities:
- Design and maintain cloud infrastructure
- Implement and optimize CI/CD pipelines
- Automate deployment processes and infrastructure provisioning
- Monitor system health and performance
- Troubleshoot production issues and incidents
- Implement security best practices
- Document infrastructure and runbooks
- Participate in on-call rotation

Education:
Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or equivalent experience

Benefits:
Competitive salary, equity, comprehensive benefits, flexible hours, professional certifications support
