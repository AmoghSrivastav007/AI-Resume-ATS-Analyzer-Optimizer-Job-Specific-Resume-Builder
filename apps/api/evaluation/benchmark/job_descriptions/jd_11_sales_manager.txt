Sales Manager

Company: SalesPro Solutions
Location: Dallas, TX (Hybrid)
Type: Full-time

About the Role:
Lead our sales team to drive revenue growth. You'll manage a team of account executives, develop sales strategies, and build relationships with key clients.

Required Skills:
- 6+ years of B2B sales experience with 2+ years in management
- Proven track record of exceeding sales quotas
- Experience with CRM systems (Salesforce, HubSpot)
- Strong leadership and team coaching abilities
- Excellent negotiation and closing skills
- Understanding of sales methodologies (SPIN, Challenger, MEDDIC)
- Data-driven approach to sales analytics and forecasting
- Strong presentation and communication skills
- Experience with enterprise sales cycles
- Ability to build and maintain C-level relationships

Preferred Skills:
- Experience selling SaaS or technology products
- Knowledge of sales enablement tools
- Familiarity with sales automation platforms
- Experience with partner and channel sales
- Understanding of marketing and demand generation
- Experience managing remote sales teams
- MBA or advanced business degree

Responsibilities:
- Lead and mentor sales team of 5-10 representatives
- Develop and execute sales strategies and plans
- Set quotas and track team performance
- Conduct deal reviews and pipeline management
- Build relationships with strategic accounts
- Collaborate with marketing on lead generation
- Forecast revenue and report to leadership
- Recruit, train, and develop sales talent

Education:
Bachelor's degree in Business, Marketing, or related field

Benefits:
Base salary plus uncapped commission, equity, health benefits, 401k, car allowance, President's Club
