Chief Technology Officer (CTO)

Company: InnovateTech Corporation
Location: San Francisco, CA (Hybrid)
Type: Full-time, Executive

About the Role:
Lead our technology organization as CTO. You'll set technical vision, build engineering culture, and scale our platform to support hypergrowth. Reports to CEO.

Required Skills:
- 12+ years of technology leadership experience
- 5+ years in senior engineering leadership (VP Engineering, CTO, or equivalent)
- Strong track record of scaling engineering teams (50+ engineers)
- Deep technical expertise across full stack and cloud infrastructure
- Experience with SaaS product development and operations
- Strategic thinking and business acumen
- Excellent communication with technical and non-technical audiences
- Experience fundraising and presenting to investors and board
- Strong understanding of security, compliance, and data privacy
- Proven ability to attract and retain top engineering talent

Preferred Skills:
- Experience at high-growth startups (Series B-D)
- Track record of successful product launches
- Experience with M&A and technical due diligence
- Knowledge of AI/ML technologies
- Published technical thought leadership
- Computer Science or Engineering degree from top-tier university
- MBA or executive education

Responsibilities:
- Define and execute technology strategy and roadmap
- Build and lead engineering, product, and data organizations
- Drive technical innovation and R&D initiatives
- Ensure system scalability, reliability, and security
- Collaborate with CEO and leadership team on business strategy
- Manage technology budget and resource allocation
- Present technical vision to board and investors
- Build engineering culture and talent pipeline
- Oversee technical operations and infrastructure
- Stay ahead of technology trends and competitive landscape

Education:
Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or Engineering required; advanced degree preferred

Compensation:
$300K-$400K base salary, significant equity package, executive benefits, relocation assistance
