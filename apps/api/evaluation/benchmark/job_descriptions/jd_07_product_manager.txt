Product Manager

Company: ProductCo
Location: New York, NY (Hybrid)
Type: Full-time

About the Role:
We're looking for a strategic Product Manager to own our core product features. You'll define product vision, prioritize roadmap, and work cross-functionally to deliver customer value.

Required Skills:
- 4+ years of product management experience
- Strong analytical and data-driven decision making skills
- Experience with agile methodologies (Scrum, Kanban)
- Excellent communication and stakeholder management
- Ability to write clear product requirements and user stories
- Experience conducting user research and usability testing
- Proficiency with product management tools (Jira, Asana, Productboard)
- Understanding of software development lifecycle
- Strong prioritization and time management skills
- Customer-focused mindset

Preferred Skills:
- Technical background or CS degree
- Experience with B2B SaaS products
- Knowledge of SQL and data analysis
- Familiarity with A/B testing and experimentation
- Experience with product analytics tools (Mixpanel, Amplitude, Google Analytics)
- Understanding of UX/UI design principles
- Experience managing products at scale (100K+ users)

Responsibilities:
- Define product vision, strategy, and roadmap
- Gather and prioritize product requirements
- Work closely with engineering, design, and marketing teams
- Conduct user research and competitive analysis
- Write detailed product specs and user stories
- Monitor product metrics and iterate based on data
- Present product updates to leadership
- Manage product launches and go-to-market strategies

Education:
Bachelor's degree in Business, Computer Science, or related field; MBA preferred

Benefits:
Competitive salary, equity, comprehensive health benefits, 401k, professional development, hybrid work
