Data Scientist

Company: Analytics Labs
Location: Chicago, IL (Remote)
Type: Full-time

About the Role:
We're seeking a Data Scientist to extract insights from complex datasets and build predictive models. You'll work on problems spanning customer analytics, forecasting, and optimization.

Required Skills:
- 3+ years of data science experience
- Strong proficiency in Python (Pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn)
- Solid understanding of statistical methods and machine learning algorithms
- Experience with data visualization (Matplotlib, Seaborn, Plotly)
- Proficiency in SQL and database querying
- Experience with A/B testing and experimental design
- Knowledge of supervised and unsupervised learning techniques
- Ability to communicate complex findings to non-technical stakeholders
- Strong problem-solving and analytical thinking
- Experience with Jupyter notebooks

Preferred Skills:
- Experience with big data tools (Spark, Hadoop)
- Knowledge of deep learning frameworks (TensorFlow, PyTorch)
- Familiarity with cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, Azure)
- Experience with time series forecasting
- Knowledge of causal inference techniques
- Experience with business intelligence tools (Tableau, Looker, Power BI)
- Advanced degree (Master's or PhD) in a quantitative field

Responsibilities:
- Analyze large datasets to identify trends and patterns
- Build and deploy predictive models
- Design and analyze A/B tests
- Create data visualizations and dashboards
- Collaborate with product and engineering teams
- Present findings to stakeholders and leadership
- Document methodologies and results
- Stay current with latest data science techniques

Education:
Bachelor's degree in Statistics, Mathematics, Computer Science, or related field; Master's preferred

Benefits:
Competitive salary, stock options, health insurance, remote work, conference attendance, learning budget
