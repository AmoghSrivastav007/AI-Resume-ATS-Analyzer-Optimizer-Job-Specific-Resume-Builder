QA Engineer

Company: QualityFirst Software
Location: Austin, TX (Hybrid)
Type: Full-time

About the Role:
Ensure product quality through comprehensive testing strategies. You'll design test plans, automate tests, and work closely with development teams to catch bugs early.

Required Skills:
- 3+ years of QA or test automation experience
- Strong understanding of software testing methodologies
- Experience with test automation frameworks (Selenium, Cypress, Playwright, or Puppeteer)
- Proficiency in at least one programming language (Python, JavaScript, or Java)
- Knowledge of API testing tools (Postman, REST Assured)
- Experience with test planning and test case design
- Understanding of CI/CD pipelines and integration
- Familiarity with bug tracking systems (Jira, Linear)
- Strong analytical and debugging skills
- Excellent attention to detail

Preferred Skills:
- Experience with performance testing (JMeter, K6, Locust)
- Knowledge of security testing practices
- Familiarity with mobile testing (Appium, XCUITest, Espresso)
- Experience with BDD frameworks (Cucumber, SpecFlow)
- Understanding of accessibility testing
- Knowledge of database testing and SQL
- Experience with load and stress testing

Responsibilities:
- Design and execute comprehensive test plans
- Develop and maintain automated test suites
- Perform functional, integration, and regression testing
- Identify, document, and track defects
- Collaborate with developers on bug fixes
- Conduct exploratory testing
- Review requirements and provide testability feedback
- Monitor test coverage and quality metrics
- Participate in release planning and deployment

Education:
Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or related field

Benefits:
Competitive salary, stock options, health insurance, flexible schedule, professional development, hybrid work
